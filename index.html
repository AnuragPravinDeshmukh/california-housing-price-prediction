Exploratory Data Analysis

California Housing Market Insights

Key statistical relationships discovered during dataset exploration.

Median Income vs. Median House Value

Median income exhibits the strongest positive correlation (r = 0.69) with housing value. Notice the distinct horizontal ceiling at $500,001 representing capped values in census recording.

Geographic & Density Distribution

High prices cluster strongly along the Pacific coastline and major urban hubs (San Francisco Bay Area and Los Angeles / Orange County), with circle diameters indicating district population size.

Median House Value Distribution

Right-skewed distribution with a median around $179,700 and a high concentration of luxury districts capped at the maximum value threshold of $500,000.

Attribute Correlation Matrix

High collinearity exists between rooms, bedrooms, population, and households, making feature engineering ratios (e.g. rooms per household) essential for model quality.
